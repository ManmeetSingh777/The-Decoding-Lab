# Addendum 27: One worked observation-to-image trace and adversarial audit drills

All film-like details in this document are **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLES**, not observations from any of the six reference videos. This document teaches the software's decision *procedure*; it must not be imported as source-style knowledge.

## A. One example: whole-character face + environment on a view change

**Original desired output (synthetic):** a completely new friendly underwater robotics engineer, illustrated at a 3/4 angle, holding a luminescent specimen, within a coherent layered underwater lab; it later turns to face the viewer. The Director/user supplies only these ORIGINAL semantic contents. The learned skill, not the Director, must provide artistic treatment.

**Evidence packet (synthetic only):**
- `W-A01`: source-image state 1, illustrated face/body/environment viewed at a particular angle; record WHOLE frame, actual actor silhouette, face/eyes/garment relationships and environmental finish.
- `W-A02`: consecutive frames through a viewpoint/acting change; record what *visibly* changed together; do not assert a private 3D/2D workflow as fact.
- `W-B01`: an unlike actor in a different visual world; identify variations and exceptions. An observation in one source character is not a universal artistic ratio.

**Weak derived statement (reject):** "Their eyes are round and the character has a large head; use bright cyan and make the actor look like Kurzgesagt." This copies or hallucinates source-specific details and does not define a producing method.

**Potential conditional construction finding (candidate until actual source supports it):** "When a new expressive figure is shown at an oblique viewpoint, whole head silhouette, positions and visible areas of near/far facial features, neck/shoulder connection, hand/prop overlap, garment boundary and focal-value separation must be designed as one coherent illustrated state. The details vary by the original actor's anatomy, role and action; do not apply one actor's head ratio to all actors." This still needs *real source witnesses, conditions, artwork and producer operation* before becoming frozen.

**New original manufacturing plan (requires actual implemented operations):**
1. Resolve typed original content fields: character role, novel appearance concept, new anatomy/gesture, original world, camera angle, action and prop. These are NOT learned aesthetic clauses.
2. Resolve source-derived conditional decisions for whole-actor form/face design, coherent environment style, material/lighting treatment and their dependencies. Each decision has real contrast/counterexample references, a frozen clause/template and operation.
3. Generate a **whole canonical actor and world concept** via the actual provider using selected approved style visual references. Save the raster bytes, dimensions, provider settings and request hash. Do not claim style mastery from a placeholder image.
4. Edit/generate 3/4 actor and frontal face/pose states **conditioned on the approved canonical actor**, together with complete hand+prop contact art and preserved costume identity; save relevant alternate drawings and clean plates.
5. When necessary, prepare real masks/foreground/background layers; verify the image parts can recombine into the approved whole drawing, not a tube-limb SVG substitute.
6. For the animated camera turn, use appropriate alternate drawings, scene graph and timing. Source style choices control *how artwork looks*; user story choices control *what happens*. Re-inspect any ungoverned visible decisions and propose revisions only through source-grounded skill updates.

## C. The completeness auditor must find this subtle case

Fixture includes correctly implemented rules for `CHARACTER_FACE_ACTING`, `DEPTH_VIEWPOINT`, and `MATERIAL_LIGHT_FINISH`, *each individually marked supported*. However, **no rule specifies how the face's near/far feature artwork changes together with light/shadow when the viewpoint turns**. A naive checklist passes all three categories. Addendum 27's relationship and reverse-construction review MUST mark a real missing cross-link and prevent promotion of the combined face-turn-and-light capability. It must derive one exact question addressing the missing joint style decision, not repeat three generic categories.

**Another fixture:** all 18 visual dimensions marked inapplicable and all links missing. Refuse `READY_FOR_ORIGINAL_IMAGE_TRIAL` because the original artwork's actual visual requirements contradict those entries. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires a reason grounded in the scope and evidence; it is not a way to silence missing observations.

**Another fixture:** a junior model suggests "metallic teal eyes with tiny diamond-shaped sparkles" without source evidence or approved brief requirement, then inserts it directly in a `SKILL.md` template. The observation→skill provenance gate must classify `MODEL_SUGGESTED_UNVERIFIED` and keep the revision RESEARCH_ONLY. The compiler should never see this clause as frozen art authority.

**Another fixture:** image provider adds a beautiful unexpected highlight absent from the skill's controlled instructions. The audit can say a highlight appeared, but it cannot prove the provider's hidden internal causal reasoning. Record as `PROVIDER_GENERATED_UNDETERMINED`, inspect whether it matters and whether a repeatable source-supported rule should be researched; don't retroactively pretend it was learned.

**These are engineering test fixtures only.** They prove missing dependency discovery and provenance wiring *if actually implemented and executed*; they cannot prove correct interpretation of professional source visuals or professional-quality image generation.
