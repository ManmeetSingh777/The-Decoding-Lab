# ADDENDUM 27 — Complete Visual-Model Investigation, Dynamic Gap Closure and Source-to-Image Provenance

**Status:** Mandatory, ADDITIVE to VME v1.3 and Addenda 24, 25 and 26. **Do not replace or edit the previous documents.** This document governs the *completeness of the visual construction model before skill promotion* and the *provenance of all artistic choices through the image provider and rendered result*. Preserve the previously approved 13-part architecture, every-frame 15+1 selection, review-before-animation, portable semantic resume and first-run offline-build/audit-only requirements. Where earlier wording sounds like "one gap only" or "Q0–Q7 questionnaire", interpret it as one **controlling next question per unresolved dependency**, within a **comprehensive, multi-relation visual-model coverage pass**. The first round may investigate many connected questions; it must not approve a partial visual model just because one question was answered.

## 0. What this actually solves (and what cannot be guaranteed)

Previous instructions can deeply answer an *already recognized* question. They do not yet guarantee that an important dimension of illustrated artwork, perspective or motion is recognized as missing. Add a **Visual Model Completeness Investigator** that actively searches for unmodeled visible construction and interdependencies before allowing a skill bundle to be called production-ready.

**Goal:** From exact, authentic source frames and temporal evidence, derive the *conditional artistic decisions* and executable production operations needed to construct unfamiliar original illustrated subjects/worlds in the targeted visual language, including the material decisions required for changing viewpoint, drawing state and motion. Do not copy source object identity, source dialogue, original scene layouts or logos.

**Two kinds of completeness must NEVER be confused:**
- `SOURCE_COVERAGE`: which *actual source frames* have been given to a vision model, inspected at sufficient resolution, and represented in semantically linked records? A full range can be frame-complete without being artistically understood.
- `PRODUCTION_DECISION_COVERAGE`: for a *declared original output and its required capabilities*, is every consequential artistic construction decision specified in a suitable skill and real operation, with evidence, applicability and controlled image/animation inputs? This can be complete **for a specified scope** while remaining incomplete for unseen subject classes or events.

Do **not** claim all possible artistic knowledge or perfect human-equivalent visual understanding is solved. The completeness investigator can expose missing branches and falsify unjustified completion; it cannot mathematically prove absence of every possible unknown. Say `SCOPE_COVERED_WITH_KNOWN_LIMITS` when appropriate, never `UNIVERSAL_STYLE_MASTERED` from a paper checklist.

## 1. Establish the COMPLETE visible-result model BEFORE asking only one narrow question

There are three linked output views for each selected source sequence and each new original scene:
1. **Whole visual image:** what the scene looks like as a complete illustrated composition at the viewer's display scale, including visually important actors, worlds, objects and finishing.
2. **Changing visual image:** what happens at every *actual* consecutive frame—what stays, what is newly drawn, what is changed, what moves, what overlaps, and what changes with camera, scale and depth.
3. **Constructible visual image:** which original source-independent assets, design decisions, art states, image provider operations and animation operations are required to create an equivalent *visual-quality class* for an unfamiliar subject.

The system cannot produce `PRODUCTION_DECISION_COVERAGE=READY` if it has only (1), only (2), or only (3). It must also relate them: `whole_image -> parts/layers/material -> viewpoint/motion states -> combined event -> producing operation`.

### 1.1 Completeness dimensions: REQUIRED ATTENTION, NOT HARDCODED ANSWERS

This table describes the **kinds of visual phenomena the investigator must search for**. It is neither a claim that each scene contains all of them nor the exact questions to ask. Each line must receive one of `RELEVANT_SUPPORTED`, `RELEVANT_UNKNOWN`, `NOT_VISIBLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_REASON`, `PENDING_SOURCE`, or `CONTRADICTED`, with evidence/uncertainty, dependencies and a downstream production consequence where relevant. The model MAY discover new dimensions not listed: it MUST create an extension entry instead of discarding unexpected phenomena.

| Dimension to actively look for | Specific important observations to make *when visible* | Required producer connection |
|---|---|---|
| `WHOLE_FRAME_LANGUAGE` | Overall illustration finish; how major form, visual density, colour roles, world layers and focal relationships work *together*; intentional simplicity versus richness | Whole-image art plan and skill rules controlling scene-wide coherence |
| `ORIGINAL_SUBJECT_DESIGN` | What makes *different* objects/organisms/machines/planets/structures feel related yet subject-appropriate: outer shape vocabulary, meaningful interior forms, part organisation, purposeful ornament and simplification | Original asset generation decision branches conditioned on subject, scale and role |
| `CHARACTER_WHOLE_DESIGN` | Full silhouette, shape masses, head/body/hand/foot relationships, stylized appeal, species/body-type adaptation, accessories and dress as one design rather than separate primitives | Canonical whole-character art recipe and identity asset |
| `CHARACTER_FACE_ACTING` | Whole head contour, near/far facial geometry, eye/eyebrow/pupil shapes and positioning, mouth design, expression families, gaze, relation of face to posture and viewpoint | Matched face/head drawings linked to identity, expression and camera/view changes |
| `CHARACTER_BODY_HAND_CONTACT` | Actual drawing of shoulder/torso/limb joins, hands/fingers/feet, overlap, weight, gesture, grips, held props, clothing continuity; full pose not tube skeleton | Canonical-conditioned complete pose, hand+prop art, contact states, alternative whole poses, anchors/layers after approval |
| `SHAPE_SURFACE_DETAIL` | Large-to-small designed visual masses, edge/contour treatment, internal accents, micro-detail placement, deliberate missing detail, part-specific treatment by importance | Shape/detail construction and bounded edit method, not arbitrary numerical geometry |
| `COLOUR_RELATIONS` | Colour ROLE across foreground, actors, materials, environments, focal region; local vs lighting colour; gradients where visible, saturation/value balance across completely different palettes | Conditional colour relationship clauses and actual reference-guided image operations |
| `MATERIAL_LIGHT_FINISH` | Material differences, highlight/shadow design, occlusion, glow, transparency, atmosphere, local light effects, how those choices depend on object parts and viewer focus | Material-aware art generation and effect/layer operations that preserve whole-scene visual unity |
| `WORLD_ASSEMBLY` | What makes a forest/city/planet/interior/biological world a designed *place*: near/mid/far structures, meaningful diversity, scale and repeating systems, negative space and connected environmental dressing | Whole-world first art construction, guided edits, foreground/background and clean-plate asset plans |
| `SUBJECT_WORLD_INTEGRATION` | Why actors/objects look native to the same illustration: shared light/colour/scale/material/edge/depth and overlaps; how focal priority is maintained | Scene-wide style decisions shared by asset, world and character skills |
| `DEPTH_VIEWPOINT` | Perspective and projected object relationships, viewpoint changes, near/far visibility, parallax, screen/world-space movement, zoom and reframe | Layer/world/camera structure and view-dependent art-state creation |
| `ART_STATE_TRANSFORMATION` | When geometry truly changes on screen; morph, revealing/occluded subparts, drawing substitution, changing scale/detail level; entry/exit world designs | Starting, intermediate and ending original drawings, appropriate code vs image-edit animation routes |
| `MOTION_ACTING` | Timing, anticipation, actions, holds, acceleration/deceleration, expressive weight, secondary movements, continuity of visual quality | Event/pose state sequence and real runtime timing rules; not just fps statistics |
| `SIMULTANEOUS_CHOREOGRAPHY` | Coexisting actor/object/environment/effects motions, cause-and-effect, contact dependencies, animated background, stage relationships and attention control | Shared multi-actor event graph; timing/control over independent and dependent layers |
| `TRANSITIONS_VISUAL_GRAMMAR` | Scene joins, cuts, match changes, reveals, wipes, scale worlds, complex effects and how artwork changes across boundaries | Multi-shot transition/artwork recipe; continuity and scene-boundary plan |
| `GRAPHICS_EXPLANATORY_VISUALS` | Visual diagrams, arrows, labels, text, pictograms, illustrative metaphors, scientific graphics and their integration with artwork when shown | Conditional graphics generation/layer composition and screen readability |
| `VISUAL_AUDIO_TIMING` | Where narration/SFX/music *visibly* correspond to appearances, reactions, cuts or motion; unknown audio when not supplied/inspected | Timing interface, not a substitute for separate creative-direction or sound-learning system |
| `FINISH_AND_READABILITY` | Cohesive polished look at actual viewing scale, focal hierarchy, clutter/simplification, artefacts, shape continuity and render/compositing defects | Final-art plan, compositing expectations and production routing, not a numeric 'beauty gate' |

**Every relevant item must be investigated with cross-links.** Example: `CHARACTER_FACE_ACTING` is not covered if facial states are described but their dependencies on head viewpoint, shoulders, lighting and simultaneous action are absent. An aesthetic observation without a practical receiving method is `SOURCE_SUPPORTED / OPERATION_MISSING`, not completed.

### 1.2 The RELATIONSHIP sweep: must capture more than 18 isolated rows

For each sequence, actively identify relationships that cause whole-result quality:
- **Part-to-whole:** silhouette ↔ articulated internals ↔ materials ↔ decorative detail ↔ surrounding world.
- **Identity over states:** canonical actor/world ↔ changed view/pose/expression/scale ↔ occlusion ↔ lighting ↔ clean-plate/background.
- **Depth and attention:** focal character/prop ↔ supporting scenery ↔ camera ↔ parallax ↔ foreground blocking and clarity.
- **Concurrent action:** active subject ↔ other subjects ↔ prop contacts ↔ secondary movement ↔ effects ↔ timing.
- **Transition between worlds:** source complete illustration ↔ reveal/transformation ↔ target complete illustration; include intermediate necessary artwork, not just camera speed.
- **Cross-subject identity:** what *varies* for different organisms, human figures, worlds and machines while maintaining a recognizable art language; track intentional counterexamples (e.g. deliberately simplified diagram).

`DEPENDENCY_NOT_OBSERVED` is a substantive gap even when each individual art category is supported. Add new links found from real footage; the six above are search seeds, not exhaustive assertions.

## 2. Exact per-sequence and per-frame observing operation

Follow Addendum 25's genuine frame-by-frame VLM ingestion and Addendum 24's crash-safe semantic handoff. Add the following mandatory **reasoning output sequence** to the vision investigator and its persisted state:

**Step A — First read the COMPLETE image and its likely high-impact visual organization.** Point out what attracts attention and the constructed relation among the most visually important illustrated forms and the world. Avoid adjectives without identifying their visual mechanisms. An actor's face is judged with its whole head and body, not in isolation.

**Step B — Enumerate visible art systems, not only semantic nouns.** For important foreground, actor, world and effect systems, record whole drawing, subparts, art treatment, layer relationships and the role they play in the complete design. Do not mistake 'scientist', 'planet', 'robot' for knowledge of how the scientist/planet/robot was illustrated.

**Step C — Compare the ACTUAL neighboring frame pixels.** Record change/unchanged states and together/independent changes. A VLM request must include every actual target frame (mapped to its verified ID/PTS/hash); if image budget constrains packet size, lower packet size, not temporal coverage. Distinguish `OBSERVED_DIFF` from `INFERRED_3D`, `INFERRED_CAMERA`, `INFERRED_NEW_DRAWING`, and `UNKNOWN`.

**Step D — Explain visible relationships causing a particular high-salience quality.** Identify what a weaker generic version would lack; cite frame spans/regions including negative/contrasting examples. Don't assert proprietary source software or unobserved intent.

**Step E — Map the observed relation to the potentially missing NEW-SUBJECT decision.** State what production method would need to *choose*, what conditions change the choice, and which reference/artifact helps. If no producing consequence follows, classify as contextual source detail, not an automatically high-impact rule.

**Step F — Run a same-sequence completeness sweep.** For each dimension from §1.1, record visible/not-visible/unknown/contradicted status and exact witness. Then ask: 'Which visually significant element, treatment or simultaneous interaction in this sequence has not been explained by the current art/construction model?' Search outside the listed dimensions and create a new entry if needed. Review at first-frame, central event, ending frame, and especially every detected novel art/event boundary. This complements—not replaces—the every-frame observation records.

**Step G — Run a cross-sequence conditional comparison.** For important choices found in a character or illustrated world, compare DIFFERENT source actors/subjects/views (preferably different films), including exceptions. Identify stable choices and adaptation rules. One beautiful original insect's exact contour is evidence for an insect only, not a universal character rule.

**Step H — Produce an action.** Either (i) publish a supported original-subject conditional decision with a real generating/edit/animation operation; (ii) create a focused new source question requiring exact frame/crop/contrast; (iii) route a model/tool-capability gap to operations; or (iv) classify a low-impact detail as deliberately deferred with a visible rationale. **Never silently turn UNKNOWN into a polished-sounding instruction.**

Keep the original v1.3/25 typed per-frame records. Store a separate `DimensionCoverageEntry` and `RelationCoverageEntry` per sequence and production scope; no need to bloat every unchanged frame with repeated full theory. The complete observation is assembled from linked per-frame evidence, native crops, sequence summaries, cross-case contrasts and dynamic question answers.

## 3. How to ask THE right question without a static questionnaire

Q0–Q7 from Addendum 25 are the **eight reasoning dimensions that every answerable learning unit may need**, not eight fixed interrogations. Addendum 26's single controlling-question rule stays active. This addendum strengthens **how the first missing question is discovered**:

1. Read the *whole-scene* coverage ledger, cross-links, active original brief (or initial intended capability), actual original result if one exists, and the evidence index.
2. Identify the most consequential `RELEVANT_UNKNOWN` or `OPERATION_MISSING` or unresolved cross-link that would stop the producer from creating the intended quality **right now**. If the initial work has not produced artwork yet, select the most visually consequential unsupported source-to-art decision, not an imagined failure in a future output.
3. Locate concrete relevant source witnesses (real frame spans, native crops and unlike-actor/world contrasts); identify exactly what is visible and what remains uncertain.
4. Express the **unknown artistic choice** in decision terms: 'For a new [subject/view/material/role/action], what choice must the producer make about [whole artwork/part relation/state change] to obtain [visibly important quality]?'
5. State **how possible evidence outcomes would alter the real producing operation**. This is the test of a useful question. A question with no plausible effect on prompts, references, asset design, editing or code isn't a priority.
6. Ask **one discriminating question for the current blocking dependency**, include full visual context and exact frames/contrasts; resolve it, update the conditional decision, and **recompute the COMPLETE coverage ledger**. It may uncover several linked subquestions; track them without replacing the controlling question with a random list.
7. Before promoting a *bundle* of 2–5 connected skills, perform an **adversarial missing-decision dry run**. Have the assembler produce an art/animation operation plan for an unfamiliar typed original brief **without generating the art**. Enumerate every necessary high-salience choice. For each choice locate its responsible frozen skill decision + operation. Any important choice with no owner returns to source investigation rather than being delegated to runtime imagination.

The algorithm must not optimize for a fixed number of questions, a fictional 99% score, or the earliest non-empty cell. **One controlling question at a time** is compatible with inspecting all applicable visual dimensions and developing a multi-decision connected cluster before the first artwork creation.

### 3.1 Good/weak question drill (synthetic, NOT observations from Kurzgesagt)

- BAD: 'What kind of colours are used?' (no role, conditional adaptation or producing operation).
- BETTER: 'When the focal actor and environment have similarly saturated local colours, how do the actual source illustrations maintain readable actor/world separation across unlike worlds, including changes in light/material, and which conditional colour/light composition choices must the original-scene art recipe apply?'
- BAD: 'What is the head proportion?' (one head, one source image).
- BETTER: 'Across unlike illustrated actors, which choices coordinate whole-body mass, head/face scale, eye placement, clothing joins and view-dependent silhouette changes to preserve readable expression and attractive identity? What changes for a novel actor with different anatomy and action?'
- BAD: 'What is the camera zoom?' (disconnected number).
- BETTER: 'During the observed scale transition, what new foreground, world and actor drawings appear as the view changes, which assets move versus are redrawn, and which original art states must be created so the next scene retains coherent detail and focal hierarchy?'

The 'BETTER' questions remain candidate investigative forms; the actual question MUST use the exact available source witnesses, observed contrast and current missing decision. Do not fill with invented style facts.

After the separately authorized source study begins, perform these connected operations before declaring the first style bundle production-ready:

Index whole footage accurately; all actual frames within each selected interval must have real vision submission and individual semantic records, with native-detail followups as needed.
2. Construct a `VisualModelCoverageLedger` for all 16 sequences using §1.1 dimensions AND §1.2 dependencies, with truthful visibility/relevance statuses. Compare sources across unlike actors and world types; do not pretend all categories are present in every shot.
3. Derive a cross-film `StyleConstructionModel`: core shared decisions, conditional adaptations, counterexamples, explicit unknowns, source witnesses and an intended actual producer operation for each actionable relation. Label source-specific visual facts separately. This is a *working visual construction plan*, not a universal style oracle.
4. Build a connected first skill bundle, strongly emphasizing **professional canonical characters + expressive faces/hands/pose families**, **fully illustrated original worlds and coherent colour/material finish**, **perspective/attention transitions**, and **meaningful linked movement** where reference evidence supports them. These are areas to examine, NOT hardcoded conclusions about source style.
5. Before sending any image job, simulate an unfamiliar typed original brief through the skill resolver, creation planner and compiler. Show the resulting full-resolution original *images* and character art to user for approval; do not force an MP4 when artwork is weak. **Do not delay EVERY first original image until all five entire films have undergone expensive frame-by-frame VLM study**; the 16 source intervals are the initial intensive set, with other frames queued for eventual full study. Do not assume the initial set is automatically sufficient just because all 16 are frame-complete.

## 5. PROVENANCE: the model must not quietly invent style between observation and pixels

A skill-only prompt compiler is necessary but not sufficient; a plausible *skill itself* might contain undocumented model-authored choices. Trace provenance at **every transformation** and surface the uncontrolled remainder honestly.

### 5.1 Provenance classification for EVERY consequential artistic choice

For each `StyleDecision`, `PromptClause`, `ReferenceConditioning`, `AssetOperation`, and `OriginalArtConstraint`, record its `origin_type` and exact parents:

- `SOURCE_OBSERVED`: directly visible in a real frame span/crop; cite source frame/hash/region and how observed (NOT a claim of private method).
- `CROSS_SOURCE_INFERRED`: generalized/adapted from multiple contrasting real source examples; cite witnesses, counterexamples, conditional reasoning and remaining uncertainty. **This is inference, not directly observed fact.**
- `PRODUCER_ENGINEERING_CHOICE`: method chosen to construct the learned visual property with available tools (e.g., masks, reference-guided edit, camera operation). Cite executable tool/operation and why chosen. Don't falsely present as a Kurzgesagt source fact.
- `ORIGINAL_BRIEF_CONTENT`: approved new subject/world/action/view/material requirements from original brief, not artistic style learned from source. Record original field path.
- `APPROVED_STYLE_REVISION`: explicitly approved updated style decision based on new evidence/output; preserve reviewer, previous version and causal change.
- `MODEL_SUGGESTED_UNVERIFIED`: proposed artistic instruction with NO sufficient source support. May exist in a *candidate* research record; MUST NOT enter a frozen style job.
- `PROVIDER_GENERATED_UNDETERMINED`: output pixel/art detail for which no documented rule or edit explains the result. Do not falsely claim exact causal control of individual pixels; classify missing coverage for consequential ungoverned features.
- `UNKNOWN_ORIGIN`: inconsistent/missing lineage; BLOCK for any consequential provider-facing aesthetic choice.

Record **lineage DAG edges** `source witness -> observed relationship -> inferred conditional decision -> skill rule and prompt clause/reference role -> compiled provider-visible request -> provider output -> asset/scene part -> finished frame`. A raw source citation attached to an arbitrary model-authored clause is *not sufficient*; the trace must show how the exact clause follows from the observed relation and how it changes the actual production choice.

### 5.2 Strict separation of allowed freedom

- The **user/Director** can choose original semantic content and explicit creative constraints in typed fields. It cannot silently overwrite frozen style rules through an unstructured free-text field.
- The **research model** can propose hypotheses and candidate style decisions with visible source evidence. It has **no automatic production authority**. The decision is versioned and reviewed before being frozen.
- The **skill** owns all aesthetic instruction clauses, visual reference selection conditions, asset-manufacturing decisions and registered editing methods that target the learned style.
- The **compiler** is a deterministic resolver that binds permitted *content* fields, never new stylistic adjectives, then logs the exact compiled payload and pinned skill hashes.
- The **provider/image model** still synthesizes pixels: its internal visual choices are not fully observable. Use real approved reference imagery, intentional image/edit route and governed inputs to reduce uncontrolled deviations. Post-generation compare output to controlled intentions and mark style-relevant uncontrolled features for source/skill improvement.

If the image service accepts extra free-form fields that can affect appearance, every such field must be accounted for as a skill-owned style field, typed original content field, or fixed technical setting. **Do not claim 100% control of aesthetic results just because all submitted prompt text was compiler-owned.**

### 5.3 At TWO interfaces, require a per-decision audit

**Interface I — OBSERVATION → SKILL:** For every frozen rule show: actual source frames/crops and observed facts; conditional cross-source explanation; applicability and counterexample; what was *inferred or added by the research model*; exact style template clause, provider image references, operator and downstream decision fields; unresolved/uncertain branches. If the model authored a clause without a traceable source-supported explanation, `RESEARCH_ONLY`, not frozen.

**Interface II — SKILL → IMAGE REQUEST:** For each provider-visible aesthetic span, image reference and appearance-affecting operation parameter show: immutable `skill_decision_id`, frozen skill hash, compiler slot binding and exact final payload location. For unmatched positive aesthetic spans mark `UNAUTHORIZED_STYLE_TEXT` and BLOCK. For a missing consequential source decision mark `MISSING_STYLE_DECISION` and BLOCK. For unresolved technical provider choices mark `UNSUPPORTED_PROVIDER_OPERATION` and BLOCK.

**Interface III — IMAGE → ART/ANIMATION:** For significant output regions/states, record what was explicitly constrained by the skill, what was supplied by original content, what was generated freely by provider, what edit/crop/mask changed it, and how it ended up on the frame. This is **output traceability, not a claim of pixel-level causality**. Compare the actual visual result at source-native/detail scale as applicable; new *unexpected* attractive details are not retroactively credited to the skill without evidence.

### 5.4 The no-silent-improvisation ledger

For each original-art job emit a `StyleAuthorityReport` containing:
- `controlled_aesthetic_decisions[]`: decisive appearance choices with skill rule and request citations.
- `source_supported_but_missing_from_skill[]`: effects recognized in source but not converted to a producing method.
- `skill_present_but_not_compiled[]`: rules that should apply yet did not reach the job.
- `provider_fields_outside_authority[]`: all unowned aesthetic provider parameters, and raw prompt bypass attempts.
- `candidate_model_inventions[]`: unqualified proposed rules suppressed before generation.
- `ungoverned_output_appearance[]`: significant actual output features not plausibly explained by controlled inputs (conservative, visually reviewed, cause unknown).
- `original_content_choices[]`: allowed original subject/action fields, excluding silent style additions.
- `provider_unobservable_residual_notice`: mandatory honest statement that the image model's internal pixel decisions cannot be exhaustively attributed.

These entries drive the **next artistic question** if they identify a high-impact style gap. Never create a fake numeric '0% model creativity' or pixel-by-pixel skill ownership claim.

## 6. COMPLETE-ART-MODEL REVIEW before skill promotion (the new missing step)

Implement a real `review_visual_model_completeness(bundle, original_scope)` function/stage with a persisted `CoverageReview` result. It must examine the **whole capability scope**, not merely approve one finished Q0–Q7 answer.

**Inputs:** 16-sequence evidence coverage, source frame/delta records, cross-actor/scene comparisons, full dimension and relation ledgers, candidate source-supported style rules, actual operation/skill DAG, and a frozen original-content scope/brief family. If original output exists, also consume observed deficiencies and user review.

**Procedure:**
1. Verify source coverage honestly: frame submissions and matched semantic observations for all selected real frames; native-detail escalation where material to art decisions. Missing sixth clip is `MISSING_REFERENCE`, not a fictitious selected sequence.
2. Sweep all §1.1 dimensions for the relevant scope. For each source-visible, consequential phenomenon require actual witness, explanation, conditional rule and receiving operation (or explicit limited status). Not applicable must cite why it is not present or relevant.
3. Walk all §1.2 relationships and actual sequence event graphs, looking for connections the individual skills omitted (actor+world, face+pose, camera+art states, material+light, multi-object interactions). Any new high-salience relationship must create a ledger entry.
4. Perform **reverse construction**: starting from the intended original finished frame or event, enumerate assets, their visual construction choices, states, image model operations, segmentation/clean plates, animation/event timings and compositing. Trace every high-impact appearance decision back to a skill decision with evidence. An LLM can inspect and suggest missing decisions, but cannot silently fill them in and call it complete.
5. Perform **adversarial visual gap search**: compare reference whole-result richness/identity to the *fully specified* expected construction (later to actual output). Ask: what visually dominant feature could be left generic, independently improvised or contradictory under this plan? Name the exact feature, current owner and missing operation/evidence. Prioritize genuine visual impact; don't demand irrelevant microgeometry.
6. Report **all** relevant uncovered issues with dependency clustering. Choose the next **one** discriminating question for the earliest/highest-impact missing producer decision; resolve and rerun the full coverage review until all high-impact required decisions have a justified receiving method or the bundle is explicitly `NOT_READY` with named limitations.
7. Freeze/compile only skill capabilities whose declared scope is supported. Evidence from synthetic offline fixtures may demonstrate software wiring but NOT actual professional visual learning.

**Statuses:** `READY_FOR_ORIGINAL_IMAGE_TRIAL`, `BLOCKED_MISSING_SOURCE`, `BLOCKED_MISSING_STYLE_DECISION`, `BLOCKED_MISSING_OPERATION`, `BLOCKED_PROMPT_AUTHORITY`, `PARTIAL_CAPABILITY_ONLY`, `ARTISTIC_QUALITY_UNPROVEN`. `READY_FOR_ORIGINAL_IMAGE_TRIAL` means the proposed inputs are complete enough to try, **not** professional quality guaranteed or learned style certified.
